Composite wing-box structural study

View project plan & analysis details

Objective

Create an original, simplified wing-box model to demonstrate a documented aerospace structural-analysis workflow.

Planned model

  • Parametric wing-box geometry with upper/lower skins, spars and ribs.
  • Define representative materials and clearly state whether the structure is metallic or composite.
  • Document idealizations, supports, load cases and coordinate conventions before solving.

Planned analysis workflow

  • Geometry cleanup and shell/solid idealization appropriate to the chosen design.
  • Mesh quality checks and property/material assignment.
  • Linear static analysis, followed by an eigenvalue buckling assessment if the setup supports it.
  • Review deformation, stress and buckling modes, and discuss modelling limitations.

Project status

Planned independent portfolio project. No completed analysis results are being claimed yet.

Planned independent project

Lightweight component optimization

Baseline structural analysis followed by a constrained design study, with comparison of mass, response and design constraints across iterations.

View project plan & analysis details

Objective

Demonstrate a traceable lightweighting study on an independently designed mechanical component, comparing the baseline design with revised iterations.

Planned method

  • Define a design space, material, loads, supports and measurable constraints.
  • Establish a baseline solution and identify design variables.
  • Run a constrained optimization or structured design-iteration study, depending on model and solver capability.
  • Reanalyse the proposed design and compare mass, stress and displacement against the baseline.

Reporting

The case study will include the objective, constraints, design changes, solver checks, before/after comparison and limitations. Quantitative improvements will be reported only after results are verified.

Planned independent portfolio project; results pending.
