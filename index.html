01 / About

Practical analysis.
Thoughtful engineering.

Aerospace engineer with expertise in CAE workflows and structural analysis.

I’m an Aerospace Engineering graduate from IIAEM, Jain University, Bengaluru, with professional experience in computer-aided engineering. My work has involved preparing finite element models, supporting structural assessments and using simulation results to inform design decisions.

I enjoy the complete analysis process: understanding the physical problem, preparing a suitable model, checking assumptions, interpreting results and communicating the outcome in a useful way. I’m currently developing independent portfolio projects to demonstrate my workflow and technical approach.

My goal is to help engineering teams and clients make informed design decisions through careful modelling, transparent assumptions and clear deliverables.

Engineering support

02

Linear static, modal and buckling analysis for appropriately scoped models and load cases.

Mesh refinement studies and comparison of selected response quantities to assess solution sensitivity.
